What We Do

Our Services

From storm-damaged roofs to full exterior transformations, Ridgestone Roofing delivers complete home exterior solutions for Calgary and surrounding communities.

01
Roofing

Your roof is your home's first line of defence against Alberta's unpredictable weather. We specialize in full roof replacements using premium asphalt shingles from trusted manufacturers including IKO and BP. Our certified crews follow manufacturer installation specifications to the letter, ensuring both your product and workmanship warranties remain intact. Whether you're dealing with age, storm damage, or simply planning ahead, Ridgestone delivers a roof built to last — on time and within budget.

Request a Quote
02
Roof Repair

Cracked shingles, persistent leaks, flashing failures, or hail impact — no repair is too complex for our experienced roofing crew. We perform thorough inspections to identify the root cause and deliver lasting solutions, not temporary fixes. Our team responds quickly to emergency calls, protecting your interior from further water damage with full before-and-after documentation provided on every job.

Schedule a Repair
03
Hardie Siding

As a James Hardie Preferred Contractor, Ridgestone Roofing is certified to install the full HardiePlank® and HardiePanel® product lines. Fiber cement siding offers unmatched durability against moisture, freeze-thaw cycles, UV fading, and impact — ideal for Calgary's climate extremes. Available in dozens of profiles and factory-applied ColorPlus® finishes, Hardie siding adds lasting value and stunning curb appeal to your property.

Get a Siding Quote
04
Eavestrough & Downspouts

Properly installed eavestroughs and downspouts protect your foundation, landscaping, and siding from uncontrolled water runoff. Ridgestone fabricates and installs seamless aluminum eavestrough systems custom-bent to exact measurements on-site — eliminating joints that can leak over time. We offer a range of profiles and colours with secure hangers, proper pitch, and correctly placed downspout extensions.
